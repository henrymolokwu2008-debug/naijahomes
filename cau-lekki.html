Contemporary Architecture, Designed for Modern Living

An architectural statement on Ayo Jagun Street. Rather than a dense conventional block, the proposed development balances clean contemporary geometry and expansive glass facades with an extraordinary 85.87% unbuilt landscaped domain.

Elevated Aesthetics • Ayo Jagun Street

Sculpted Facades & Expansive Glass Fenestration

Conceived as an exclusive upcoming residential project in Lekki Phase 1, CAU Properties Limited introduces an uncompromising vision to No. 10 Ayo Jagun Street. The architectural composition combines deep bronze solar louvers with floor-to-ceiling insulated glass systems, ensuring abundant natural daylight while shielding interiors from tropical solar gain.

The 5-floor residential composition integrates floor-to-ceiling insulated glass systems, deep bronze solar louvers, and private balconies that frame tranquil outdoor views. Rather than maximizing building coverage at the expense of environment, the development sets aside approximately 85.87% of the ~885.399 sq. m site area for lush landscaped grounds, leisure decks, and open-air enjoyment, with 27 dedicated on-site parking spaces designed to protect serene pedestrian garden circulation.

  • ✓ Projecting cantilevered balconies with frameless structural glass balustrades
  • ✓ Recessed garden terraces providing natural cross-ventilation
  • ✓ Bespoke architectural screening fins for privacy and solar shading
  • ✓ Concealed ambient LED lighting emphasizing clean geometric lines at dusk
